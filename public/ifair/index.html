The iFair Journey

From its humble beginnings to becoming MIT's premier international cultural festival, iFair has been bringing together our diverse community in celebration of global cultures.

The Early Years

iFair began as a small gathering of international students sharing their cultures through food and performances. The international community at MIT, comprising about 10.12% of undergraduates and 41.95% of graduate students, found a home in this celebration.

The Golden Era

From elaborate dance performances on Kresge Oval to a global food bazaar, iFair became a cornerstone of MIT's cultural calendar. Traditional dances, music performances, and cultural exhibitions brought the world to MIT's doorstep.

A Cultural Mosaic

Through the years, iFair has showcased the rich tapestry of cultures at MIT. From Turkish folk music to Chinese fan dances, from South Asian fusion to Latin rhythms, each performance tells a unique story of heritage and identity.

iFair 2025: A New Chapter

This year, the ISA is reviving the tradition in full spirit. With over 40 cultural clubs, regional food tastings, interactive cultural booths, and a jam-packed performance schedule, iFair 2025 promises to be a celebration like never before.
